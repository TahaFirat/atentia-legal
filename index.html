Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ / SSS)

How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new device?
Open ATENTIA and tap Restore Purchases on the top-right of the summary screen or inside Settings → Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?
On iPhone/iPad, open Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → ATENTIA and tap Cancel Subscription at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I delete my local measurement data?
Open ATENTIA → Settings → Permanently Erase All Local Data to wipe all on-device IndexedDB records immediately.
