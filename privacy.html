Privacy Policy (EN)

ATENTIA: Cognitive Integrity (ch.atentia.clinical) is engineered on an offline-first, zero-server personal data architecture.

1. 100% On-Device Storage (IndexedDB)

All millisecond reaction-time trials (SART, PVT-B, Task-Switching, Prospective Memory), 6-item psychometric scale answers, 21-day rehabilitation protocol logs, and exercise metrics are stored strictly locally on your device using IndexedDB. We do not require user registration, email sign-up, or cloud accounts.

2. Zero Advertising & Zero Tracking

ATENTIA contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero cross-app trackers, and does not access Apple IDFA (NSPrivacyTracking = false).

3. Ephemeral In-RAM Screenshot Parsing (Optional)

If you optionally choose to parse an iOS Screen Time or Android Digital Wellbeing screenshot, the image is compressed client-side, processed ephemerally in volatile memory (RAM) solely to extract aggregate daily duration numbers, and immediately discarded within milliseconds without ever being written to disk or persistent cloud storage. A 100% offline manual entry form is always available.

4. Subscriptions & Purchase Verification

Subscription transactions are processed directly by Apple App Store (StoreKit 2) and Google Play Billing using anonymous store receipts without linking personal identity.

5. Right to Erasure (GDPR Art. 17 & KVKK Art. 7)

You can permanently and irreversibly erase all local records at any time in one tap via Settings → Permanently Erase All Local Data inside the app.
